# Three-minute Shwāsa demo

1. **0:00 — 2026, with a memory.** Open `/`. Select Anand Vihar. The date is 8 November 2026; this is a historical-analog outlook, not a live forecast. Explain why the large rolling-day AQI estimate differs from hourly PM2.5.
2. **0:25 — The whole festival.** Click Build-up, The burst, Hangover. Scrub to midnight. Observe the historical median and the range of earlier festivals. The actual/difference controls are disabled for the future.
3. **0:55 — A neighborhood, not one city number.** Select a different place on the real H3 map. Open Compare places. Explain that interpolation is modelled and spatial validation remains work in progress.
4. **1:20 — Look inside.** Open Understand the why. Show source years and minimum sample counts. No fake causal contribution bars for this empirical outlook.
5. **1:45 — Evidence over accuracy slogans.** Open Model report card. The analog member’s rolling 2025 festival backtest has 25.0% lower MAE than persistence. Show all years and actual historical-spread coverage; do not call the band calibrated.
6. **2:15 — Different tools for different questions.** Switch to Diwali 2025 and open What-if Lab. Perturb real regression inputs. State that statistical scenarios do not establish causal policy benefits.
7. **2:40 — Give people something useful.** Open My Air, show a lower-exposure window and the share button. End at City desk and export a planning worksheet.

Press **J** for the built-in guided tour. The demo uses bundled real historical data; no service keys are required. Map tiles have an offline schematic fallback.

## Claims to make

- “We built a working, explainable air-quality product around collected CPCB and environmental data.”
- “The 2026 outlook aligns historical seasons by Diwali, not by Gregorian date.”
- “No 2026 festival observation is fabricated. Every future actual is null.”
- “We publish errors and limitations, including a training defect we detected and corrected.”

## Claims to avoid

- “2026 has 80% calibrated confidence,” “official live AQI,” or “validated street-level accuracy.”
- “Traffic causes this fraction of pollution,” “our model guarantees clean air,” or a promised winning score.
